      🎵 Music

      Sound

      The MIDI songs' own reverb and chorus. Normal plays them as written.
      Shapes the music of MIDI, audio and video songs to the room and the speakers (not YouTube, and never the microphone). Custom shows ten bands.
      Each song's level is learned while it plays; the next time, it starts at the target, so a quiet song and a loud one sound alike. On a first play the level eases in as the song goes. Not YouTube.

      Melody guide (MIDI)

      F2 (or M) and the status line's − + step through the same five levels. Turn the melody up to learn a song, off to perform, solo to isolate it.

      MIDI

      Adds a Mixer button to the panel buttons at the bottom of the stage: volume, mute/solo and live levels per channel for the current MIDI song. MIDI songs only.
      Off: a Key change leaves the drum kit alone. On: the drums move with the key too, as before.

      Key detection

      Reads each song's key from the MIDI. Shown as current → transposed beside the title, and drives auto-tune's "Auto" key.

      Chords (guitar · MIDI)

      ⚠ Best-effort — may not be fully accurate. Chords are auto-detected from the MIDI notes (the files carry no chord data), so some songs — especially power-chord / pedal-tone arrangements — can read the wrong quality or root. Treat it as a guide. Follows the Key transpose · # sharps. Simplify collapses 7ths/sus and fixes power chords to the key's triad (e.g. E5→Em).
      🎙️ Singing

      Microphone & voice

      Feedback & noise
      Best fix for feedback: use headphones. Echo-cancel + gate tame howl on speakers.
      Latency
      How late your voice reaches the scorer. Scoring and the guide's mic trace are shifted by this much; 0 = no compensation.
      Effects
      Auto-tune
      Auto-tune bends your voice to the note. "Melody guide" snaps to the song's own melody (needs a song playing).

      Pitch guide

      Reads the guide melody from the MIDI. Overlay + scoring need the mic on.

      Score

      Scored per note against the song's own melody, octave-forgivingly. MIDI songs only, and the mic must be on. Your best per song is remembered, and every scored song gets a row in Songs → Rankings (under the guest's name, or the host singer name).
      🔎 Songs & search

      Search

      Close matches are marked ≈ and listed after the exact ones.
      A number pressed on the stage opens Songs with it in the search, listing every songbook's song with that number; Enter takes the top one. A number left alone in a panel it opened clears and closes by itself.

      YouTube search (BYOC · Chromium)

      Searches append YouTube results for "<your search> keyword" when the local library comes up short. Live metadata only; playback is the official YouTube embed.
      📱 Phones & party

      Remote (phones)

      📱 Scan to control from your phone
      Room code —
      URL —
      Shows the QR here (and on the stage: When idle = the home screen only, Always = also a small code beside the pitch guide during a song; the Songs panel's Queue tab shows the room code); phones scan it to open /remote and queue/search/control. Leave the URL blank to auto-detect. A guest's jump to the front of the queue is refused while fair play is on. Free-for-all: anyone who can reach the URL can control playback — keep it to a trusted network.

      Queue

      Fair play puts each new song at the back of that singer's own round, so everyone sings once before anyone sings twice. Songs already queued never move.
